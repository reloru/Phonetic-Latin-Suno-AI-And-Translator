    What the outputs are
    
    
    
      
    Suno Latin
    
      
    Your Latin, with long vowels kept as macrons, short u written ū (so sum is not sung “suhm”), c before e/i written k, v written w, consonantal i written y, and ae/oe written ai/oi. Example: Hīc ego sum → Hīck ehgo sūm.
    
      
    Lyric respelling
    
      
    English-style spelling as whole words: Heek ehgo soom. Use it when Suno keeps reading Latin as English or as Church Latin.
    
      
    Stressed respelling
    
      
    Hyphenated syllables with the stressed one in capitals: AHT-kweh. Capitals sometimes make Suno stumble; turn them off in Options.
    
      
    IPA
    
      
    The reconstructed pronunciation itself, for checking. It follows W. S. Allen, Vox Latina (2nd ed., 1978), and Wiktionary's Classical Latin transcriptions.
    
    
    
    
    Vowel length
    
    
    Stress and vowel quality depend on vowel length. Type macrons yourself (on iPhone, hold a vowel key), or leave them out and the dictionary fills them in. When one spelling has two readings (liber “book” / līber “free”), the more common word is chosen and the other is offered under Words. Macrons you type are always kept.
    
    
    Lyric sheets
    
    
    Lines such as [Verse] or [Chorus], and any [bracketed] text, pass through unchanged. Line breaks are kept.
    
    
    Translator
    
    
    Translations aim at Classical Latin of the 1st century BC and come back with macrons. Those macrons are checked against the dictionary before pronunciation, because translation models often misplace them.
    
    
    Use it from Shortcuts
    
    
    GET /api/convert?text=Hīc+ego+sum&format=text&output=lyric returns one output as plain text; leave out format for JSON with all four.
    
    
    Sources
    
    
    Vowel lengths come from English Wiktionary (via kaikki.org), ordered by the Dickinson College Commentaries Latin Core Vocabulary; both are CC BY-SA. The Suno-specific spellings are heuristics built from how Suno has actually sung test lines, not from any Suno documentation.
